# VISION: Omnifeed (Newz)

Omnifeed is designed as the ultimate autonomous intelligence command center — "Google News on steroids":

## Core Foundational Tenets
1. **Multi-Source Correlated Reality**:
   No single wire service, aggregator, or platform owns the narrative. Omnifeed continuously correlates breaking developments across mainstream wires (Google News, Yahoo News, MSN, Fox), community link aggregators (Reddit, Hacker News, Lobsters), and decentralized micro-commentary streams (Bluesky AT Protocol, Mastodon Fediverse).

2. **Unsupervised Clustering & Story Provenance**:
   Incoming news items are normalized, stripped of noise, and grouped using high-precision similarity metrics (Jaccard token clustering and semantic embeddings). Each topic maintains an immutable chronological narrative timeline showing how the story evolved across sources.

3. **High-Density, Zero-Fragmentation Dashboard**:
   A single-page, responsive operations center with zero fragmented subpages. High-value data (sentiment polarity pulse, live wire badges, entity tags, discussion context) is immediately visible with interactive deep-dives, dual-direction pagination, and OLED contrast options.

4. **Offline Resilience & Ubiquitous Distribution**:
   Zero hard dependencies for local development (automatic SQLite fallback), coupled with cross-platform desktop shells (Tauri v2) and mobile-ready Progressive Web Apps (stale-while-revalidate offline caching, Service Worker Background Sync coordination, and offline sync queue replay).
